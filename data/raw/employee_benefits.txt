EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

All full-time employees are eligible for company health insurance.

The health insurance plan covers the employee and eligible dependents.

Employees can add eligible dependents during the annual benefits enrollment period.

The company provides a monthly internet allowance of INR 1000 for eligible employees working remotely.

Employees who leave the company must return company equipment before their final working day.

Employees can contact the HR department for questions regarding benefits and insurance.
