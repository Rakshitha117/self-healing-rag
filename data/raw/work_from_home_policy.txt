WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.

Employees must request work-from-home approval through the employee portal at least one working day before the planned work-from-home date.

Emergency work-from-home requests can be approved by the reporting manager.

Employees working from home must remain available during normal working hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection and a suitable workspace.

Work-from-home privileges may be suspended if an employee repeatedly fails to meet performance or attendance expectations.
