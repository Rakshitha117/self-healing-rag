COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Employees are eligible for 24 days of paid leave per calendar year.

Annual Leave:
Employees can take up to 18 days of annual leave per year. Leave should normally be requested at least 3 working days in advance.

Sick Leave:
Employees are eligible for 6 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate may be required when sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive working days.

Emergency Leave:
Employees may request emergency leave for unexpected personal or family situations. The employee should inform their manager as soon as possible.

Leave Approval:
Leave requests must be submitted through the employee portal. The reporting manager is responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Carry Forward:
Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the following calendar year.

Public Holidays:
Public holidays are separate from the employee's annual leave entitlement.

Probation:
Employees under probation can take sick leave and emergency leave but annual leave requires manager approval.
