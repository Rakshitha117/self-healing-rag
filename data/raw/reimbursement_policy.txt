EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Travel expenses:
Employees can claim hotel, transportation, and meal expenses incurred during approved business travel.

Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of completing the business trip.

Meal expenses are limited to INR 800 per day.

Hotel expenses are limited to INR 4000 per night.

Employees must upload original receipts with every reimbursement claim.

Claims without supporting receipts may be rejected.

The finance department normally processes approved claims within 10 working days.
